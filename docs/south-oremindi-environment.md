# South Oremindi Mountains environment

30 September 2026. The user requested implementation of South Oremindi's terrain and wildlife after the Elfland work. The original environment preview contained no NPCs, settlements or quests. On 1 October, the user authorized [Inquest Clearlistern's small Long Tarn cottage](next-session-inquest-clearlistern.md), with a literal blank-slate resident and named mailbox; its future quest remains deferred. In the desktop game, use **F8 → Go anywhere → South Oremindi Mountains** to arrive at the southern forest foot beside North Ibenwood.

The atlas spelling is **South Oremindi Mountains**, registered as region 37. Its 45 authored cells comprise 20 high-mountain cells, 19 hill cells and six lake cells. Five connected lake cells form the long glacial basin; the isolated southeastern cell forms a separate forest tarn. There are no mapped river edges in this region, and this build introduces no rivers. The per-cell climates retain the warmer sheltered lake country as well as the cold alpine and ice cells.

[The terrain module](../src/content/regions/south-oremindi/south-oremindi-world.js) builds three unequal high summits, projecting shoulders, cirque hollows and lower wooded hills. The summits are tuned around 410–470 metres, with lake surfaces at 95 and 35 metres and shaped beds beneath them. These heights and route dimensions are implementation choices for the playable heightfield, not newly fixed lore measurements. Relief fades within the owned hexes, preserving the neighbouring Ibenwood ground.

[The scenery](../src/content/regions/south-oremindi/south-oremindi-scenery.js) places silver fir, silver birch and stone pine on suitable lower ground, with juniper and smaller trees toward a locally varying treeline around 230 metres. Short turf, shrubs, small alpine flowers, scree and scattered boulders replace dense woodland above it. Snow cover increases through the upper slopes and varies with exposure and steepness. Trees and solid rocks use the same ground and collision system as movement; natural routes and lake shores retain clearance.

Natural traverses lead from the southern approach through shoulders and benches toward the high ground, with a separate approach into the sheltered lake hollow. These are exposed stone and meadow routes, not constructed roads. Steep off-route faces use the existing climbing and fall systems. The two lakes have visible water surfaces, real submerged beds and water queries shared by swimming and wildlife.

The read-only lore sources are `../world-builder/azhora_lore/geography/regions/oremindi.md` and `../world-builder/azhora_lore/fauna/azhoran_fauna_overview.md`. The latter explicitly documents the **Oremindi snowgoat** and **mountain eagle**, plus altitude-dependent fauna and sparsely inhabited glacier zones. Their distinct rigs depict patchy grey-white wild goats and broad-winged eagles. Existing deer, boar, hares and ducks extend neighbouring woodland, turf and lake fauna; the uncertain pale-fox and unidentified creature remain unimplemented.

[Wildlife habitats](../src/content/regions/south-oremindi/south-oremindi-wildlife.js) give every hill cell resident ground animals, with additional hares on scattered upper turf benches, goats on rocky shelves, small duck groups on both lakes and three solitary eagles. Homes and identities are deterministic and remain fixed when the player travels away. The [existing wildlife controller](../src/content/regions/western-regions/west-regions-life.js) applies real collision, height, slope, water and regional ownership checks at placement and during movement. Mountain animals remember usable return routes; elapsed offscreen time does not let them teleport through a cliff or lake. Nearby groups use instanced rigs and distance culling. These animals are wild ambient fauna, not domestic stock or human residents.

Validation passed 26 focused Node checks, 78 related regression checks and 22 desktop checks, with no renderer errors. The desktop run exercised F8 travel, both lakes, developer flight and the full ascent against the rendered ground and actual colliders: about 873 metres of walking, five headwall climbs, a 470-metre summit, positive remaining stamina and no damage or stalls. All 81 animals across 49 habitats passed placement checks, and the normal saved checkpoint remained unchanged. Overview, lakeshore, woodland and snowgoat screenshots were visually reviewed. These checks establish functionality; this run was not a controlled performance benchmark.
