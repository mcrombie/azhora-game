# Northern countries and outer islands

Environment implementation - 5 October 2026.

**Later 5 October additions:** [Eshtor Plateau](eshtor-plateau.md) extends this pipeline to 34 regions and 1,052 authored hexes. [Claude's Ibenals, Henborth and Mithala city](region-reviews/main-integration-2026-10-05.md) are also integrated. The earlier 33-region scope below is retained as its implementation record.

The user requested this 33-region expansion while putting further loading optimization work on hold. Full remains the default launch mode; the experimental Fast behavior is unchanged. These are terrain and wildlife environments, with no new civilian populations or regional quest arcs.

## Scope and landscape direction

The 1,018 authored hexes retain their outlines, coastlines and river courses. The game-only corrections rename West Gorgi Mountains to **Gorgiwood**, East Gorgi Mountains to **South Gorgi Mountains**, and West/East Inseld to **West/East Ithzel**. Gorgiwood?s former mountain cells become deep forest. World Builder remains read-only.

Gorgiwood has tall, closely spaced firs, alders and beech, subdued green light, a folded ravine, fallen trunks and fungi. Gorgi?s two mountain regions have continuous folded ridges, gullies, treelines and pale summits. The open countries use grassland, heath, meadows and scattered trees; the southern island groups add palms, figs, weathered limestone and natural arches. Cold Stones is treeless. Coastal seals and dolphins live offshore; riverbank habitats can support otters and waders. Birds supplement ground populations.

## Integration

- Region IDs 1?79 stay stable; new IDs are appended as 80?112.
- Each region has a dry arrival path, three distinct named natural locations, and F8 ? Go anywhere access.
- Walking, climbing, falling, tree grounding and scenery use the same regional terrain. Trees have a registered harvestable species.
- Persistent wildlife homes are selected from dry, traversable habitat. Large herd animals get wider scenery clearances; aquatic homes stay offshore.
- Scenery uses deterministic instance batches. Each region owns its geometry resources so parking a remote region cannot invalidate another region?s tree crowns.
- Both map exports and the generated playable survey include the new names. Previously saved cartography is migrated without duplicating discovery XP.
- The chart grays all 15 unbuilt regions, including glimpsed adjacent hexes; its legend explains the gray fill. Fog of war still applies.

## Development hold

**Do not begin the southern jungle expansion without a new user instruction.** Anubrul, Haatrul, Maanub, Maawad and the other unbuilt southern regions remain map context. North and South Scythe were explicitly requested in this batch, so they are built; that does not authorize their southern neighbors. After this batch, prioritize stabilization at the current world size before proposing further expansion.

The remaining unbuilt atlas regions are: Anubrul, Barqat, Haatrul, Maanub, Maawad, Nuurat, Qadmar, Qadwaaqaad, Rihas, Riwaad, Sabrqad, Saxhan, Saxrul, Waahaat, Waahan.

## Reproducible checks

- `node scripts/build-outer-regions-atlas.mjs` refreshes the climate/river snapshot from the read-only authored atlas, applying the game naming corrections. Run after both standard map export commands, then `node scripts/build-region-survey.mjs`.
- `tests/outer-regions.test.js`: all footprints, stable IDs, terrain, arrivals, species, habitats, river/lake beds, map names and unbuilt-region gray fill.
- `tests/outer-regions-integration.test.js`: production construction of all 34 regions; 68 arrival-path traversals; tree roots/species, bounded geometry batches, and actual wildlife creation/update including sea populations.
- Cartography, survey, developer-atlas, local map detail and map-fog regression checks cover map integration and legacy discovery migration.
- Native reviews: `outer-0` (Nothwood), `outer-9` (Gorgiwood), `outer-10` (North Gorgi), `outer-14` (Central Lond), `outer-28` (Cold Stones), `outer-30` (Azhor Stones); `map-build-status` shows the built/unbuilt transition. Reviews use isolated saves.

## Limits

This is a first environment pass across a large area, not a claim that every square metre has been visually reviewed. The checks cover all region arrivals and population builders; screenshots sample representative biomes. Regional societies, cities and quest content need separate design. Further loading optimizations are deliberately deferred.

## Validation result

All 54 focused geography/cartography/atlas tests passed, as did all three production-world integration tests (including 66 traversals), plus four targeted developer/travel checks. Native screenshots reached the sampled regions with no recorded game errors; the Electron process reports a GPU shutdown message after capture on this machine.

The broader map-fog suite reports one out-of-scope assertion: Selemis?s discovery disc is only 55% inside Selemi, below its 60% threshold. The 99 newly added discovery areas pass their own ownership, description and separation checks. Selemis?s city footprint was not changed for this task.
