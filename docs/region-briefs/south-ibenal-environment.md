## Scope, natural journey and exclusions

Build terrain, mapped water, vegetation and wildlife only. Start with three to five natural features: exposed headland/strand, a sheltered stream or shallow valley, an open coastal interior, an irregular outer woodland transition, and for North the mapped hill/narrow mountain approach. Habitat choices must follow exposure, soil, drainage and the actual climate; list proposed species and lore sources before implementing them. No livestock quota, identical animal-band templates or automatic deer in every habitat. Keep at least one meaningful off-road habitat legible from normal player height.

South's principal journey should connect a safe existing Alezhor-side approach near the west stream, the new coastal interior, and a clearly documented North-facing endpoint, then return. Include real ordinary entry/exit from one mapped shallow stream where appropriate; classify a necessary swim honestly. The exact coordinates and expected walking/swimming/climbing demands belong in the brief after measuring the final base. North should continue the agreed coastal connection to its actual Oremindi-facing plain and return, without claiming an unbuilt pass is usable. A path advertised for ordinary walking must pass the real production controller both ways; do not certify it from slope samples or a nudged screening walker.

Preserve Elfland's inner defenses, permission/exceptional-stealth rules, dimensional-withdrawal reserve, hidden groves and living-tree felling prohibition. Do not cut a new highway through the forest, move its saved trees or disclose hidden settlements. Gathering fallen wood and entering elven territory remain separate permissions.

**North Ibenal does not directly border West Oremindi.** Its eight mountain edges touch South Oremindi. No West Oremindi/Sevron edits, new Sevron entrances, bypass of its challenge/treasury, or conversion of its high traverse follows from this assignment. Keep South Oremindi campaign reservations and Inquest's cottage/approaches as well. Roads, ports, farms, trade settlements, provisioners, civilians, quests and lore-owned cities are outside this environment handoff. Natural clear walking lines do not authorize constructing the lore's Mountain Road. Do not edit the World Builder repository; record atlas/lore discrepancies in game documentation.
