A traveler’s help becomes a local custom.

This reproducible demonstration starts with a finite satchel of barley and repair timber. A scripted traveler fulfills actual requests and carries messages. The simulation records every transfer, changes residents’ trust and relationships, and lets repeated assistance establish the Common Reserve.

About this demonstration

This is a saved, interactive history: browsing it makes no AI requests. The separate game integration supports daily simulation, resident dialogue, deliveries, repairs, messages, map discovery, and continuing histories per save. Public visitors can inspect the illustrated book without a collaborator login.

The pilot is proposed for Crombie’s review. Azhora and its source world lore are by mcrombie. The generated communities are a Hearthfall integration experiment; their stories are not presented as approved world canon. Prose and illustrations are AI-generated from recorded simulation facts.
