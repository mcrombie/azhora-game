Move & face
WASD / arrows. Right-drag to look; scroll to zoom.
Target focus
T locks / releases the opponent nearest your view; E cycles visible opponents. Strafe while facing your target. Cyan diamond = focus; gold ring = strike range. Right-drag temporarily overrides the follow camera.
Strike
X / left click. Face the soldier with the gold ring.
Dodge
Hold a direction and tap Space. Space alone backsteps. Release before dodging again.
Thrust
Narrow orange warning: sidestep across the attack.
Sweep
Wide pink warning: retreat beyond the blade.
Counter
After a dodge, the soldier lowers their weapon. The green ring and draining bar show the opening: close in and strike before it disappears.
Taking a hit
Each soldier hit costs 20 health. A brief pale ring marks protection from another hit: you can still move or dodge to clear the danger.
